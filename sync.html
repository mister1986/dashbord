We’ve improved how your EQUINET appointments sync to your phone’s calendar. The Calendar sync screen now shows you the sync status of every appointment, so you can see at a glance that your day is on your phone and never miss a job.
1

Send the appointment, get it confirmed

Send your appointment through EQUINET. When your client confirms, you’ll see a green Confirmed by… tag on it.
2

Switch the toggle to ‘On’

Open Calendar sync and turn on Synchronize schedule to device. That’s it. Works on both iPhone and Android.

iPhone

Syncs to Apple Calendar

Android

Android Calendar or Google Calendar, your choice
3

NEW

See your sync status in real time

The progress bar shows how many appointments are on your phone’s calendar, plus when it last synced. When it reads Every appointment is on your calendar, you’re set.

Good to know

•  Appointments sync once your client has confirmed them.

•  Only appointments from today onwards are added. Past ones stay in EQUINET.

•  On Android, pick an EQUINET calendar that stays on your phone, or your Google account so jobs show on all your devices.
