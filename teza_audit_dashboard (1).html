The macro story
2025 ran on a single-objective, lead-form playbook with broad audiences and one-off creative production. 2026 introduced Catalog Ads, Sales / Purchase campaigns, layered retargeting, and the 3-Hook Rule applied as a launch precondition. The account shifted from "running ads" to running a creative system.

FUNNEL EVOLUTION
Same audience saw the same creative all year long in 2025 — that ended in 2026
In 2025, a cold viewer and a 5-time site visitor were essentially in the same audience pool, seeing the same ads, with no exclusion lists configured. In 2026 each stage has its own campaign, its own creative purpose, and its own exclusion rules — converters drop out of TOF, video-completers move into MOF retargeting, site visitors become the BOF + catalog audience.

Findings, Weaknesses & Roadmap

What was earned · what's still missing · what to do next

Δ Synthesis

Wins · what 2026 got right

WIN
Catalog-driven retargeting layer
Adding the product catalog and using dynamic catalog ads at the BOF/RT layer is the single highest-leverage addition. It lets Meta personalize SKU exposure by viewer signal.
WIN
Sales objective unlocked deeper bidding
OUTCOME_SALES with pixel + CAPI gives Meta a richer optimization signal than form-fill alone — letting the algorithm hunt actual quote-readiness rather than vanity leads.
WIN
Audience exclusions in place
Converter-exclusion lists stop the same person from seeing the same TOF ad ten times after they've already become a customer — a hygiene fix that was completely absent in 2025.

Weaknesses still remaining

GAP
Testimonial inventory is still thin
Even with creator-sourced + customer content added in 2026, the testimonial library is small relative to other verticals. MOF retargeting is bottlenecked by repeat-exposure of the same 3–4 testimonials.
GAP
LP message-match isn't audited per concept
Many 2026 ads still point to a generic homepage rather than concept-matched LPs. Meta ad headlines and LP heroes diverge — a known conversion-rate killer.
GAP
CAPI deduplication isn't verified
Both pixel and CAPI fire — but without proper event_id deduplication, OUTCOME_SALES optimization may be ingesting double-counted events, hurting bidding signal quality.
