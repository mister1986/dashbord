This account's rate is roughly 2–3x the typical benchmark, independent of the lead-quality problem above.
Niche benchmark check (Meta lead ads, dental)
Published 2026 dental benchmarks cite Facebook/Meta lead-ad conversion rates around 8–15% for social-sourced dental leads reaching a booked appointment, with well-run campaigns converting form-to-appointment near 50%. This account's Meta channel is at 0.4% reaching scheduled — dramatically below even the low end of the published range, not just "a bit soft."

Prioritized recommendations

HighStop or completely rebuild Paid Social (Meta) lead ads. 96.9% "not interested" across 261 leads and 8 consecutive months, with 0 patients started, is not a tuning problem — it's a structural mismatch between the ad/offer and lead quality (likely broad targeting, no qualifying question in the lead form, or an offer that attracts non-buyers). If it's already been paused since April, keep it off until the form/targeting is rebuilt against the benchmark range (8–15% social lead conversion) rather than restarting the same setup.
HighAdd or tighten geo-targeting on any Meta campaign before relaunching it. 67% of Paid Social's leads carry an out-of-state phone area code, a rate 2–4x every other channel — for a single-location practice, that strongly suggests the ad set isn't geo-restricted tightly enough (or is running placements/audiences outside the practice's actual service radius). This is a likely primary driver of the 0% conversion rate above, and a quick, checkable fix before any other rebuild work.
HighFix the appointment show-rate problem separately from lead sourcing. 43.6% no-shows vs. a 15–20% published benchmark is costing appointments regardless of which channel generated the lead. Confirmation calls, SMS reminders, and online self-scheduling (shown in industry data to lift show rates substantially) would compound with any lead-quality fix above.
MediumClose the attribution gap. 125 leads (19%) have no recorded source. Until that's fixed, a fifth of the funnel is invisible to this kind of analysis — add a "how did you hear about us" field at intake or fix lead-source tracking in the ad platforms/forms.
MediumInvest more deliberately in what's already working. Direct (15.7%) and Organic Search/GMB (12.1%) convert leads to patients 3–4x better than the account average with no ad spend — reviews, local SEO, and referral prompts are the highest-confidence lever available right now.
LowRevisit Paid Search (Google). It's inconsistent month to month (11.8% to 80% "not interested") rather than uniformly bad like Paid Social — worth a closer look at which specific campaigns/keywords are driving the bad months before writing off the channel.
